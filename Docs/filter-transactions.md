## Filter transactions by account (Budget tracker)
- Give a filter list on top of the transactions to show only the transactions of the chosen accounts.
- More than one account can be selected; **All accounts** clears the filter.
- A transfer is shown when either its *from* or *to* account is selected.
- The period's Income / Expense / Net and the per-day totals follow the filter.
- Chips use the liquid glass effect; a selected chip uses the Accent button style (primary tint, white text).
